What reads at 1/20

the study · world/TRAITS-NEW.md "Shoes"
  • Scale. A shoe is 5 × 9 × ≤ 6 voxels (25 × 45 × 30 cm) under a 16-voxel head on a 10-voxel leg. From the street camera a voxel is ~5 px at 10 m and ~2.5 px at 20 m, so only the silhouette and two or three value blocks survive; laces, tabs and marks are for the pfp.
  • The shoe is the plinth. The big head wants a wide base: 5 wide plus a 1-voxel outboard flare on chunky soles, keeping the 2-voxel gap between the feet.
  • Midsole = the street read. 1 row (court, skate), 2 (runner), 3 (tank, platform), always in a value that breaks from the upper, over a dark 1-row outsole.
  • Toe spring. The front sole voxel lifts one row: it reads as a rocker from the side and keeps the toe off the ground at toe-off (the toe pivots at z 6).
  • Collar ≤ y 5. Low y 3, mid 4, high 5. Over the ankle (z −2…2) y 3–5 rides the shin; the toe box and heel ride the foot; y ≥ 6 would ride the thigh and shear at the knee.
  • Pants eat the collar. Stacked denim covers y ≥ 2 over z −3…3 and shorts pull tube socks over x −4…−2, z −1…1, so the identity lives in the sole, the toe box, the heel and the outer face. Tongues sit at z 2, in front of the shin.
