# Mainland B · ROOT ACCESS (collectors' enclave)

**The idea.** The island is the grind; the Mainland is the payout. You step off SECOND CHANCE, the island's own ferry, pass the MAINLAND terminal and walk a palm allee to a brass ROOT ACCESS wall and a guard. Inside is SUDO CT, a manicured loop around a giant pink cursor that clicks the fountain.

Each home is a modernist flex: a flat roof, breeze block, a bright door, a glass garage with the car on show, a pool and a sculpture. The head house has a gold 3× statue of its owner's agent. Open lots show an AVAILABLE board and a chalked ghost footprint, so claiming land reads.

**What scales**
- A lot is a frame (position and angle) plus a home type (slab, butterfly, cantilever, vault, A-frame) with its own colour, door, car and sculpture.
- A court is a stem plus 5–8 radial lots round an island piece. New courts branch off a spine road, each with its own gate and name.
- A court opens with AVAILABLE lots, and each claim turns a ghost footprint into a home.
- Every court shares the hills, the island and the ferry.
