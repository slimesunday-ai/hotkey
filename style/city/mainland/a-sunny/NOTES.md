# Mainland · A · Sunny Springfield cul-de-sac

**Idea.** The Mainland is the island's reward. You step off SECOND CHANCE under a WELCOME TO THE MAINLAND arch, pass the ice cream truck on Harbor Rd and turn into Sunny Ct, a curving court that ends in a bulb with a blossom tree on its island.

Six houses sit on pie lots facing the turnaround: the Springfield (a hoop over the garage), a Cape, a porch Gable-front, a Ranch (kids selling lemonade), a Gambrel and the Trophy House (a gold cup on the roof). Two staked lots, AVAILABLE and FOR SALE, make claiming land read at a glance. AI City and the jail lighthouse haze on the horizon.

The walls are sherbet: brighter than Pleasantview, never pastel. Roofs, grain, light and fog match the island.

**What scales.**
- A lot is data (kind, front point, angle). Houses are built in lot space and stamped at any angle, so a new bulb is a centre plus N angles.
- A neighbourhood is one court curve, one bulb and one ground pass.
- Streets branch off Harbor Rd (Maple Loop is stubbed), and far houses run into the haze.
- Upgrades are parts: porch, dormer, hoop, pool, trophy.
