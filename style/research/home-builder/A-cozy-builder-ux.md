# Cozy Home-Decorating UX — Research Brief

Research for AI City's home-decorating mode (trailers/apartments/houses/penthouses, 0.25m voxel grid, 90° rotation, dollhouse view under consideration). Every claim is sourced inline; anything unconfirmed is marked UNVERIFIED.

## 1. Thirteen mechanics that make decorating feel good

**1. Categorized picker.** Club Penguin's catalog splits into floor/room/wall/pet tabs (https://clubpenguin.fandom.com/wiki/Furniture_%26_Igloo_Catalog); Habbo opens a dedicated "Free furni" panel in build mode (https://help.habbox.game/hc/en-us/articles/8169258497181-How-to-build-in-your-room). Cuts search time as the catalog grows.

**2. Favorites / recently-used — largely absent.** No title researched documents an explicit favorites or recently-used shelf (UNVERIFIED/likely absent across ACNH, Sims 4, Habbo, Bloxburg, Toca Life World). Unsolved territory here.

**3. Ghost/validity preview.** Sims 4 highlights an item's footprint green (valid) or red (invalid) before you commit (https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/Build_mode); Stardew Valley does the same green/red tile check (https://gamerant.com/stardew-valley-how-to-rotate-furniture/). Answers "can I put it here" before the click, not after.

**4. Grid-snap with an escape hatch.** ACNH snaps to a hidden tile grid, clean but prone to "awkward gaps" and blocked fine adjustment (https://acnhchill.com/animal-crossing-new-horizons-full-guide/interior-design/decorating-fundamentals/); Bloxburg's grid toggles on/off (H) and warns off-grid placement causes gaps (https://welcometobloxburg.wiki/controls/build-mode/); House Flipper 2 adds a finer "Precise Placement" sub-grid alongside normal snap (https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3176141226); Paralives calls its wall grid "optional," not absent, despite fan sites claiming "no grid ever" (https://www.paralives.com/development). Speed and confidence from the grid; an escape hatch for whoever it frustrates.

**5. Wall, ceiling and stacked-surface layers.** ACNH furniture sits on the floor, mounts to a wall, hangs from the ceiling, or stacks on other furniture (https://animalcrossing.fandom.com/wiki/Furniture_(New_Horizons)); Habbo has dedicated stacking furniture ("Z-Shelves") for players who "like to stack" (https://habboxwiki.com/Furni). Multiplies usable space without more floor area.

**6. Rotate (and flip).** Near-universal: ACNH rotates with one button press (https://nookipedia.com/wiki/Designing); Bloxburg has a dedicated Rotate sub-mode in its Transform Tool (https://welcometobloxburg.wiki/controls/build-mode/); Toca Life World flips items horizontally (https://toca-life-world.fandom.com/wiki/Home_Designer). The cheapest way to make one asset fit many contexts.

**7. Direct-manipulation drag, with repeat-place.** Pocket Camp's drag-and-drop is called "super user-friendly" (https://twinfinite.net/guides/animal-crossing-pocket-camp-how-to-customize-your-camper/); Sims 4 lets you hold Shift to repeat-place an item without reopening the catalog (https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/Build_mode_(The_Sims_4)). Dragging beats a menu since the object visibly follows the cursor; repeat-place removes the tax of filling a shelf with many of one item.

**8. Undo/redo and multi-select.** Sims 4 binds Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y, marquee-drags its Select tool over several objects, and Shift-drags to delete a batch (https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/Build_mode_(The_Sims_4)); ACNH's R button selects multiple items to move as one (https://nookipedia.com/wiki/Designing). A named gap elsewhere: Bloxburg's wiki warns "there is no unlimited undo stack — delete carefully" (https://welcometobloxburg.wiki/controls/build-mode/). Undo makes experimentation feel safe; multi-select speeds up restructuring instead of nudging one item at a time.

**9. Recolor over an eyedropper.** Sims 4's "Design" tool (R) recolors or re-patterns one material channel for free (https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/Buy_mode, https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/Create_a_Style); Townscaper lets you paint block color live while building (https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/how-townscaper-works-a-story-four-games-in-the-making); Paralives promises a full color wheel across furniture, lighting and textures (https://www.paralives.com/development). No title documents a literal eyedropper tool — an open idea here. High-leverage for a voxel game: one mesh, several palettes, no new models.

**10. Camera: orbit vs isometric, and cutaway walls.** Sims 4 toggles walls Up/Cutaway (hiding near walls)/Down (https://bestgamingtips.com/sims-4-change-wall-view/) and adds a build-only top-down view (https://teamoverpowered.com/guides/sims-4-build-mode-guide-for-beginners/); House Flipper defaults to first-person, and HF2 added an optional top-down "sandbox" camera (https://www.pcgamer.com/house-flipper-2s-sandbox-mode-basically-looks-like-the-sims-build-mode-in-first-person/). Players want more: an EA thread asks for per-room cutaway, not whole-perimeter (https://forums.ea.com/discussions/the-sims-4-feedback-en/add-walls-cutaway-view-by-room/12291321). No source describes a literal roof-and-front-wall-off dollhouse camera in any game researched — genuine white space for AI City, not a pattern to copy.

**11. Sound and animation "juice."** Unpacking ships 14,000+ unique foley sounds and won a Game Developers Choice Award for Best Audio (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unpacking_(video_game)); Tiny Glade matches a sound to every action — stacking bricks, filling a pond — which reviewers call central to its feel (https://www.shacknews.com/article/141431/tiny-glade-review-score). Turns placement into a small reward, not a silent data-entry step.

**12. Collision handling that keeps players in flow.** Stardew Valley explains why placement failed (too close to a wall, an obstruction) via a red-tile state (https://gamerant.com/stardew-valley-how-to-rotate-furniture/); Tiny Glade skips refusal by reinterpreting input as valid — a path meeting a wall becomes an archway (https://www.pcgamer.com/games/city-builder/tiny-glade-wont-stop-delighting-me-with-new-discoveries/); Townscaper's solver "fails silently," tolerating odd results rather than stopping the player (https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/how-townscaper-works-a-story-four-games-in-the-making). Never leave the player stuck.

**13. Touch controls — thin evidence, one clear pattern.** Toca Life World is drag-to-place, re-drag-to-reposition, tap-to-flip, built for touchscreens from the ground up (https://toca-life-world.fandom.com/wiki/Home_Designer); Bloxburg gates finer touch nudging behind a paid "Advanced Placing" pass, implying default mobile placement is coarse (https://welcometobloxburg.wiki/controls/build-mode/). Even mouse/controller placement is unsolved industry-wide — a Dreamlight Valley Steam thread is titled "Furniture placement controls are terrible" (https://steamcommunity.com/app/1401590/discussions/0/3832046780609479669/).

## 2. What to avoid

- **Grid too rigid, no override:** Sims 4 players ask on official forums to disable/loosen snapping (https://forums.ea.com/discussions/the-sims-4-gameplay-en/re-how-to-disable-snapping-on-build-mode/8615184); the community's real workaround is the `bb.moveobjects` cheat (https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/The_Sims_4/cheats).
- **No move tool, only rotate/eject:** Habbo's own dev team admitted the system "only allows players to eject or rotate furni" and had to add a move function back (https://www.habbo.com/community/article/28535/improving-building-in-habbo).
- **No/weak snapping:** House Flipper 1 retrospectives call shelf-alignment "incredibly frustrating" with no real snap tool (https://thirdcoastreview.com/2020/06/12/game-review-house-flipper/), fixed in HF2.
- **No safety net:** Bloxburg's absent undo stack (https://welcometobloxburg.wiki/controls/build-mode/) raises the cost of every mistake.
- **Whole-house-only cutaway:** Sims 4 players want per-room wall removal, not whole-perimeter (https://forums.ea.com/discussions/the-sims-4-feedback-en/add-walls-cutaway-view-by-room/12291321).
- **Camera discomfort:** House Flipper's first-person camera caused enough motion sickness to become a tracked feature request (https://houseflipper2.canny.io/mechanics-features/p/motion-sickness-camera-fixes).
- **Inconsistent sets:** Stardew Valley fans say the furniture system is "here to ruin your day" since pieces rarely share a footprint or set-mate; the same piece notes some decor won't stack, making bulk decorating tedious (https://screenrant.com/stardew-valley-furniture-decorations-problem/).
- **Mismatched expectations:** reviewers warned Pocket Camp players expecting mainline-ACNH depth "will only lead to frustration" (https://toucharcade.com/2017/11/21/animal-crossing-pocket-camp-review/).
- **Requirement-gating as the only mode:** Hello Kitty Island Adventure only moves a Friend in if the cabin's theme matches their taste (https://hellokittyislandadventure.wiki.gg/wiki/Furniture) — good for characterization, but homework if the only way to decorate.

## 3. Ownership and meaning

- **Show without risk:** ACNH's Dream Suite lets others visit an uploaded snapshot of your island; nothing they do there touches the real island (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20200728005579/en/Nintendo-News-Fireworks-Shows-Dreaming-and-More-Make-Their-Way-to-Animal-Crossing-New-Horizons). Decouples showing off from being griefed.
- **Judged showcase:** Habbo runs recurring Room Design and Fashion competitions (https://www.habbo.com/community/article/37105/winners-announced-olympus-room-design-competition, https://www.habbo.com/community/article/28808/habbo-fashion-design-competition) — decorating becomes a legible, timestamped achievement.
- **Frictionless visiting:** Club Penguin toggles an igloo public/private, and a friend's card jumps you to their igloo, even offline; a Like button made rooms socially scored (https://clubpenguin.fandom.com/wiki/Friends_List, https://cpjourney.net/news/2024/02/igloo-experience-update-511).
- **Multiplayer visiting as explicit intent:** Dreamlight Valley's post-launch "ValleyVerse" lets friends tour each other's landscaping (https://www.pcgamer.com/disney-dreamlight-valley-multiplayer/).
- **Discoverable sharing at scale:** Sims 4's Gallery lets anyone upload/search/download whole builds by item, hashtag, or creator ID (https://help.ea.com/en/articles/the-sims/the-sims-4/gallery/) — the genre's most-copied "show your work" system.
- **Unlocks tied to relationship, not just money:** Dreamlight Valley ties furniture and "motifs" to friendship-quest progress with a character (https://www.thegamer.com/disney-dreamlight-valley-furniture-unlock-sets-pieces-guide/) — decor marks how the relationship is going.
- **Decorating *for* someone else's taste:** Hello Kitty Island Adventure's taste-matching requirement (https://hellokittyislandadventure.wiki.gg/wiki/Furniture) is the closest documented analogue to "build the home for your agent" — the space visibly belongs to whoever you built it for.
- **Rarity as status:** Habbo furni is tiered — Norms, Club, Rares, Supers/Ultra Rares — tradability visually flagged (https://habboxwiki.com/Furni); owning, not just arranging, is status.

## 4. Three directions

**A — Animal Crossing-simple.** *Loop:* pick up any owned item, place it instantly on a snapped floor/wall/ceiling layer, rotate, done — no separate build mode, no fail state. *Controls:* click/tap-to-place on a highlighted valid cell, one rotate key, multi-select for clusters. *Cost:* low-medium — one grid-snap solver, three placement layers, validity highlighting; no free-transform math. *Needs from objects:* footprint plus floor/wall/ceiling placement flags per voxel model — fits the existing 0.25m grid directly.

**B — Sims-deep.** *Loop:* enter a dedicated build mode, free-position with an optional grid, recolor via swatches, toggle a cutaway camera, undo/redo everything, share finished homes gallery-style. *Controls:* drag with a snap-to-grid toggle plus a precise sub-grid mode, dedicated rotate/move/select/delete tools, marquee multi-select, per-object recolor. *Cost:* high — undo/redo, marquee multi-select, a per-material recolor pipeline, an ideally per-room wall-visibility state machine (Sims' own players want better than Sims has). *Needs from objects:* material variants or a shader recolor path per model, finer collision volumes than one grid cell.

**C — Habbo-social.** *Loop:* decorate mainly to be seen: patrons visit, rare/unlockable items function as status, public/private toggles or competitions surface the best homes. *Controls:* reuse Direction A's placement; the added complexity is social/economic, not manipulation. *Cost:* medium, shifted into new systems: home-to-home visiting, a rarity/tier tag, a social layer (likes, comments, timed competitions). *Needs from objects:* a rarity/tier tag and an acquisition method — bought, earned, or stolen — not unconditional access.

**Recommendation.** AI City is a spectator sim where the human is a patron, not the resident, and decorating already sits beside stashes, safes and burglary — full Sims-depth (B) is disproportionate cost for a side activity. Best fit: **A's cheap, no-fail placement carrying C's meaning layer** — showcase, visiting, rarity-as-loot plugged into the existing crime economy (decor as flex, as target, as something an agent reacts to). Keep B's ideas — recolor, per-room cutaway — as later upgrades, not launch requirements.
